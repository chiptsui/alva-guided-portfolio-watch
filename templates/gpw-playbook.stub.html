Understanding your watchlist

This watch reviews publicly-available financial statements and news for the companies on your watchlist. It focuses on developments that matter over multi-year time horizons — not short-term price moves.

What does an alert mean?

An alert means a material business event has been detected — such as a significant earnings surprise, a major regulatory decision, or a strategic acquisition. It is a prompt to read the linked evidence and decide whether your long-term view of the business has changed. It is not a buy or sell instruction.

What do revenue, net income, and free cash flow mean?

Revenue is total sales — all money received from customers. Net income is profit after every expense including taxes. Operating cash flow is actual cash generated by running the business (excludes non-cash accounting items). Free cash flow is cash left after spending on long-lived assets (factories, equipment). Positive and growing free cash flow generally indicates a financially healthy business.

How current is this data?

Financial statement data is sourced from structured filing databases and may lag the most recent earnings announcement by days. The retrieval date is shown next to each company's data. For real-time developments, check linked news sources in any alert.
